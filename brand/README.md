# Pixel Frame brand

Formerly The Million Pixel Burn ($PIXEL). The mark is a pixel in a frame, drawn as a P.
Every asset here is generated by `python3 brand/build.py` (needs `rsvg-convert` and ImageMagick). Edit the grids there, not the outputs. It also writes `apps/web/lib/brandPaths.ts` and the web icons.

## Grid

The mark is 11×13 units. The hole is the rect (3,2)–(8,7) and is transparent; the centre pixel is the rect (4.5,3.5)–(6.5,5.5).
In the lockup the mark, wordmark and ticker units are 30 : 5 : 3, as measured from the source.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** two mark units on every side (for the lockup, 2/11 of the mark's width).
- **Minimum size:** mark 11×13 px, which is the 16 px favicon. Wordmark 127×14 px. Lockup 318 px wide (the wordmark at 2.5 px per cell is the smallest that holds).
- **Scale by whole multiples of a unit** wherever the output is pixels. The centre pixel needs an even unit to land on device pixels; the favicons are hand-fitted for this.
- **Always on black.** The cream pixel and the transparent hole need a dark ground, so on any other colour put the mark on a black tile (`mark-on-black.svg`). Don't recolour, outline or round it.

## Intentionally not renamed

These are internal or on-chain, and renaming them would break something or rewrite history:

- The Anchor program and module `pixel`, its crate and directory, the `PixelError` type, and all PDAs and seeds
- The `@mpb/*` package names, and the `mpb_test` test database
- The GitHub repo `applicure/pixelburn`, including the `source_code` URL in `security_txt`
- The Coolify project and app names `pixelburn`, `pixelburn-web`, `pixelburn-api`, `pixelburn-indexer`, `pixelburn-keeper`, `pixelburn-db`
- The DB name and user `pixelburn`
- `PIXELBURN_ADMIN_TOKEN`
- The devnet mint: symbol `PXTEST`, name `Pixel Burn DEVNET TEST`, and its ids. They are on-chain, as are the defaults in `chain/scripts/pump-devnet.ts` and `chain/scripts/devnet-report.ts`, which reproduce them
- The historical records `chain/DEVNET.md` and `chain/devnet-log.json`
- The hosts `pixelburn.applicure.io` and `api.pixelburn.applicure.io`: still live until the cutover, and kept after it as redirect and alias (`docs/domain-cutover.md`)

"Burn" as a mechanic stays too (`/burns`, buybacks), as does lowercase "pixel" as a unit.
